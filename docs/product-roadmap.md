## Operating rule

- Verify the exact purchased product before production.
- Ask the owner only for experience details that cannot be verified from the
  purchase record or official product information.
- Do not publish unused Select candidates until official information, current
  reviews, risks, and disclosure wording are complete.
- Popularity is discovery evidence, not proof that LIFT should recommend a
  product.
