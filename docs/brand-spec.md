# Brand Spec v0.1

## Confirmed

- Brand: LIFT
- Tagline: 毎日を30秒ラクにする。
- Audience: people who want to make everyday life a little easier.
- Core categories: household goods, parenting products, gadgets, and PC accessories.
- Selection priorities: time saved, value for money, design/usability, quality, and share-worthiness.
- Voice: calm, concrete, honest, and useful.
- Visual direction: generous whitespace and restraint, with a small amount of practical excitement.

## Trust rules

- “買ってよかった” is reserved for products actually used by the owner or family.
- Researched products must be labeled as researched, not personally tested.
- Every reviewed product includes an “惜しい点”.
- Unsupported savings, time, performance, and popularity claims are prohibited.
- Estimates must state their assumptions.
- Affiliate benefit never overrides product suitability.

## Exclusions

- extremely low-quality products;
- questionable supplements;
- gambling-related products;
- information products;
- counterfeit or copy products;
- exaggerated claims.

## Editorial idea

LIFT begins with a recurring frustration, not a product category. The preferred framing is:

```text
small frustration -> useful insight -> suitable product -> honest trade-off
```

## Approved logo

The production logo is stored at `assets/brand/lift-logo-transparent.png`.
It is the owner-supplied transparent PNG containing the circular mark, upward
arrow, `LIFT`, and `30 sec`. The renderer must use this asset and must not
re-create the mark from text.

## Open decision

The final bundled Japanese font files have not been selected. The renderer
currently uses the system font fallback defined in the design specification.
